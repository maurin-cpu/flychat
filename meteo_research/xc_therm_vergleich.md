- avg_climb = 2.38 * 0.50 = **1.19 m/s** (XC-Therm: 2.3)

- w*_deardorff = ((0.034) * (0.163) * 900)^(1/3) = 1.71 m/s

- Pre-Cumulus: Unser Modell passt gut (+/- 20%)

- Unterschiedliches Wettermodell (ICON-D2 vs ICON-CH1) erklart weiteren Teil
